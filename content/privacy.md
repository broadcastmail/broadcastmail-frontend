## What we collect

We collect only what it takes to run the product.

| Data | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Your account | Your name, email address and Supabase organization ID, received when you sign in with Supabase. |
| Credentials | Your Supabase secret key and Resend API key. Both are encrypted at rest and never shown again after you save them. |
| Recipient data | The email addresses and columns your filters select from `auth.users` and your profile tables, read when you preview or send a campaign. |
| Campaigns | Subject lines, HTML bodies, filters, and the delivery status of each recipient. |
| Billing | Your plan and billing history. Stripe handles card details, so we never see or store them. |
| Usage logs | IP address, browser and request logs, kept to debug errors and stop abuse. |

## How we use it

We use your data to run BroadcastMail: building audiences, rendering previews, sending campaigns through Resend, reporting delivery, and billing you. We email you about your account, such as failed sends, receipts and changes to these terms. We don't send marketing email unless you opt in.

We don't sell personal data. We don't share it with advertisers, and we don't use your users' data to train models.

## Your users' data

For the people in your Supabase database, you are the data controller and we are your processor. We act only on your instructions: the filters you set and the campaigns you send. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to email them and for honoring unsubscribes. Every campaign includes an unsubscribe link for this reason.

A Data Processing Agreement is available on request.

## Sub-processors

| Service | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| `Supabase` | Authentication and our application database |
| `Resend` | Email delivery, using your account |
| `Stripe` | Payments and invoicing |
| `Vercel` | Application hosting |

We'll update this list at least 14 days before adding a new sub-processor.

## Retention

Per-recipient delivery records are kept for 90 days, then deleted. Campaign content is kept until you delete it. When you disconnect a project, its keys are deleted immediately. When you delete your account, everything else is removed within 30 days, apart from billing records we're legally required to keep.

## Security

Keys are encrypted at rest with AES-256, and all traffic uses TLS. Database access uses a read-only role. Staff access to production is limited and logged. If a breach affects your data, we'll tell you within 72 hours of finding it.

## Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data at any time. Most of this is in Settings. For anything else, email us. If you're in the UK or EU, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. If one of your users contacts us directly, we'll pass the request to you.

## Changes

If we make a material change, we'll email you at least 14 days before it takes effect. The date at the top of this page always shows the latest version.
